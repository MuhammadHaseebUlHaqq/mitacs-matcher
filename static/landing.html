// QUESTIONS 005

Where do my documents go?

Nowhere. The whole thing runs on your own machine, and your files are only ever sent to the AI provider you choose with your own key. There is no account and no server holding your CV.

Do I need to pay for anything?

You need an API key from OpenRouter or Google Gemini. Both have free tiers that are enough for this. The tool never charges you anything itself.

What if I add more documents later?

Add them any time and rebuild. Your uploads stay put between visits, and pages that were already read are not read again — so adding one document does not mean starting over.

What if it stops halfway?

Free API tiers have per-minute limits, so a long run can be interrupted. Everything already done is kept — press the button again and it picks up where it stopped rather than starting from the beginning.

Can I trust the scores?

Treat them as a strong first pass, not a verdict. Every score comes with the evidence behind it so you can check the reasoning yourself, and the honest gaps are shown alongside the fit. Read the projects before you apply.

Does this apply for me?

No. It produces your shortlist and an exportable summary. You still apply through the Mitacs portal yourself, and you still need at least three different provinces across your choices.
